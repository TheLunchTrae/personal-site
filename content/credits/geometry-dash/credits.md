Fan-made Geometry Dash homage. Not affiliated with or endorsed by RobTop Games.

Geometry Dash is a trademark of RobTop Games. Apart from its title lettering, this theme's art is drawn in code, and it plays no sound.

## Fonts

- Body text: [Geist and Geist Mono](https://github.com/vercel/geist-font) by the Geist Project Authors at Vercel, under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](/licenses/geist-OFL.txt).
- Headings and labels: [Pusab](https://www.fontsquirrel.com/fonts/Pusab) by Ryoichi Tsunekawa ([Flat-it](http://flat-it.com/)), a freeware font under the [Flat-it license](/licenses/pusab-flat-it-license.txt).

## Title lettering

The Geometry Dash title on the home page tour was made with [GD Colon](https://gdcolon.com/)'s Geometry Dash font generator. It uses the Oxygene 1 lettering by Jakob Fischer ([Pizzadude](https://www.pizzadude.dk/)) as drawn in the game, with textures by RobTop Games.
